# Demo Route

## Best 7-screen investor-style flow

1. `/`
Open the homepage and pause on the hero, trust strip, and featured listings.
Talk track: this is a managed marketplace for premium used bikes with inspection and escrow.

2. `/marketplace`
Show the catalog, filters, and curated browsing state.
Talk track: buyers can browse verified supply instead of raw classifieds.

3. `/listing/1`
Open a high-quality listing detail page.
Talk track: this is the decision page, where trust, seller quality, and pricing signal come together.

4. `/transaction/1`
Move into the managed transaction flow.
Talk track: reserve, inspection, payment, and delivery are framed as one guided workflow.

5. `/inspection/1`
Show the inspection report as proof before funds move.
Talk track: the platform reduces trust risk by turning condition into a structured artifact.

6. `/seller`
Switch to the seller workspace.
Talk track: sellers do not just upload listings; they manage listing quality, orders, and payouts from one workspace.

7. `/admin/analytics`
Finish on the ops side.
Talk track: the marketplace is not only a storefront, but also a moderated operating system with analytics, approvals, disputes, and inspection capacity.

## Optional wow extensions

- `/admin/approvals`
Use this if you want to emphasize moderation and serial verification.

- `/inspector/inspect/assign-1`
Use this if you want to show field-operations depth and evidence capture.

- `/admin/disputes`
Use this if your lecturer cares about business realism and trust/risk handling.

## Short presentation script

- Problem: used bike marketplaces feel risky and unstructured.
- Solution: VeloTrust adds inspection, escrow, guided transactions, and operational oversight.
- Buyer value: safer discovery, stronger trust, better decision support.
- Seller value: cleaner listing workflow and a more premium selling environment.
- Platform value: moderation, inspection, and dispute systems make the marketplace believable.
